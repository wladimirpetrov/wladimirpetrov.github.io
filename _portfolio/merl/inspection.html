Multi-drone inspection at MERL

Bring coordinated inspection missions into a real-drone experimental pipeline.

Connecting research plans to real flightRobotics Engineer Intern · Jan–Apr 2025

The problem

An inspection team must cover targets in a constrained environment while accounting for obstacles, separation and the need to recharge.

My work

During my MERL internship, I implemented, integrated and experimentally validated multi-agent trajectory planning and tracking for Crazyflie inspection missions. I set up and maintained the ROS2/CrazySwarm2 stack and integrated trajectory execution, tracking, visualization and testing.

Context & evidence

I configured SLAM Toolbox for independent localization, generated 2D maps for subsequent point-cloud representations, and ran RViz2 and hardware experiments. The diagram is an original mission illustration; it is not footage of the MERL experiment or an output of the paper’s planner.

Project focus

  • ROS2 and CrazySwarm2 integration
  • Real-drone trajectory execution and tracking
  • Localization, mapping and experimental validation
