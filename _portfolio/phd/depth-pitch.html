Depth & pitch control near the surface

Track a commanded depth while reducing unnecessary actuator activity in waves.

L1 / LQR for underwater vehiclesIFAC 2024 · Ocean Engineering 2025

The problem

Near the surface, waves and slower hydrodynamic disturbances affect both depth and pitch. Correcting every wave can make the actuators work unnecessarily.

My work

I developed and evaluated an L1-augmented LQR autopilot for the Joubert BB2 underwater vehicle. The design addresses persistent depth errors and reduces actuator response at wave frequency.

Context & evidence

The published studies use numerical simulations. This detailed explainer introduces the near-surface problem, separates slow tracking error from wave-frequency actuator activity, explains the architecture, and compares the roles of nominal LQR, frequency shaping and L1 reference augmentation. Its motion and traces are illustrative, not paper data.

Project focus

  • Depth and pitch regulation
  • Slow-disturbance compensation
  • Reduced actuator activity at wave frequency
