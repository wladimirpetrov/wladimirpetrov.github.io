Depth & heading control for AUVs

Reach a new depth and heading while working within the vehicle’s actuator limits.

L1 / MPC for constrained maneuversOcean Engineering · 2026

The problem

A near-surface underwater vehicle must maneuver with uncertain hydrodynamics and control effectiveness that changes with forward speed. Commands must remain compatible with physical actuator limits.

My work

I developed the control methodology and software for a Joubert BB2 depth–heading study, with simulation validation at two forward speeds. The study compares tracking under model mismatch while checking actuator magnitude and rate limits.

Context & evidence

The study is published in Ocean Engineering (2026), volume 368, article 128400. The detailed explainer covers model mismatch, adaptive reference management, physical actuator constraints, the choice of an infinite horizon, continuous-time domain transformation, Bernstein transcription into a nonlinear program, convex-hull geometry and the repeated MPC update. Method background: Fahroo and Ross (2008), for infinite-horizon domain transformation; Bernstein transcription is a separate step. The reported cases use calm water and a nonlinear six-degree-of-freedom simulation plant at two forward speeds. Animation traces are illustrative, not numerical results.

Project focus

  • Commanded depth and heading changes
  • Actuator magnitude and rate limits
  • Fast-transit and lower-speed simulation cases
