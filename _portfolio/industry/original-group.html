Lead the mechanical development of industrial robotic systems.

Original-group PJSC · MoscowChief of Mechanical Engineering Team · 2020–2022

The problem

Developing industrial robots brings together mechanical design, dynamics, structural analysis and the engineering documentation needed to turn designs into buildable systems.

My work

I led a 24-person mechanical engineering team responsible for R&D, mechanical design, simulation and engineering documentation. The work covered Delta, articulated, Cartesian, SCARA and polar robotic systems.

Context & evidence

My engineering tools included Autodesk Inventor, Siemens NX, Teamcenter and ANSYS, with work on motion, dynamics, stress and deflection. The illustration represents the field of work; it is not a company design or a delivered product.
